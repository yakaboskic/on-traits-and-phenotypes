# Discussion

## What the representation makes explicit

The framework distinguishes an observable function from its evaluated value and from a profile of many values. Composition records deterministic constraints such as the BMI equation. Restriction records which traits were actually observed, and context extension states when a richer description preserves existing evaluations.

These distinctions support a precise account of phenotype equivalence. Sharing a label, sharing a value type, agreeing in one cohort, and agreeing as functions are different claims. A vocabulary can identify the subject matter of a measurement while leaving its operational construction unspecified.

## Limits of the present formulation

The deterministic core assumes compatible domains, types, and admissible operations. Measurements with noise require observation events or probabilistic operators. Longitudinal data require time-indexed states or trajectories. Partial functions require applicability conditions that remain separate from recording indicators.

Extensional equality does not preserve derivational provenance. The expression graph must therefore remain available alongside its interpretation. Conversely, a difference in recorded construction does not by itself prove that two interpreted functions differ.

The ontology diagram is a historical working artifact. Its release, edge conventions, and counts require validation before use as empirical evidence. The original universal claim about corpus-based distances has been replaced by the explicit counterexample in the supplement.

## Empirical and causal work still required

The proposed resource audit needs a reproducible sampling frame, pinned resource versions, declared roots and operations, and a documented procedure for distinguishing explicit, recovered, and unknown derivations. Counts and depth distributions should not be reported until that audit is performed.

The causal follow-up requires separate identification assumptions and proofs. A compositional translation can preserve operations without guaranteeing causal transportability. Source confounding, study selection, trait availability, and intervention-specific measurement changes must be addressed explicitly.

## Conclusion

Representing traits as typed observables gives phenotype descriptions an explicit evaluation semantics. Retaining their construction makes deterministic dependencies and observation context available for scrutiny. This provides a foundation for comparing phenotype definitions while leaving empirical validation and causal identification as substantive scientific tasks.
